Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version: I don't collect your personal information.

What I collect

Nothing. My apps and this website have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I don't know who you are or how you use my apps.

What stays on your device

Anything you create or save in an app (for example streaks, entries or custom questions) is stored on your own device. I never receive it, and deleting the app or clearing your browser data removes it.

Third parties

My web games and this site are hosted on GitHub Pages, and my iOS apps are distributed through the Apple App Store. Those services may log basic technical information (like your IP address) under their own privacy policies. I don't receive or control that information.

Children

I don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share or sell.

Changes

If this ever changes, I'll update this page and the date above before any new data collection begins.

Consent

Because I don't collect personal data, there is nothing to opt in or out of, and no cookie banner to click through.

  • This site and my apps don't use cookies for tracking.
  • Your device may store small bits of data locally so the app works offline and remembers your progress. That data never leaves your device.
  • If I ever add a feature that needs your data, I'll ask for your clear permission first, and you'll be able to say no.

By using my apps you're agreeing to the terms below. If you don't agree, please don't use them.
